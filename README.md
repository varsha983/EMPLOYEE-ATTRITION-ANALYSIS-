# Employee Attrition Analysis & Predictive Modeling System

An end-to-end Machine Learning and HR Analytics project built to analyze employee turnover patterns, evaluate predictive retention models, and deliver an interactive decision-support dashboard for people operations teams.

---

## 1. Project Objective & Problem Statement

Employee turnover imposes severe organizational costs—averaging 50% to 200% of an employee's annual salary when factoring in recruiting expenses, onboarding overhead, knowledge loss, and team disruption. 

The primary objectives of this project are:
1. **Analyze Workplace Drivers**: Identify statistical and demographic patterns associated with voluntary employee departures across departments, roles, compensation tiers, and engagement scores.
2. **Train & Compare Supervised Models**: Benchmark **Logistic Regression**, **Decision Tree Classifier**, and **Random Forest Classifier** using Scikit-Learn pipelines to identify at-risk employee profiles while addressing severe class imbalance (~16% positive rate).
3. **Deliver an Interactive HR Analytics Dashboard**: Provide an intuitive, multi-view Streamlit application enabling HR leaders to explore workforce distributions, inspect model diagnostics, and simulate individual retention scenarios.

## 5. How to Reproduce the Analysis & Train Models

Run the complete pipeline to perform data validation, execute the EDA, train and cross-validate all three algorithms, generate diagnostic charts, and save the champion pipeline:

```bash
python train.py
```

Optional arguments:
- `--test-size 0.20`: Held-out test split ratio (default: 20%).
- `--seed 42`: Random seed for reproducible stratified splitting.
- `--skip-eda-figures`: Fast execution without re-rendering PNG figures.

## 6. Model Evaluation & Genuine Benchmark Results

All models were evaluated on a held-out, stratified test set of **294 employee records** (247 retained, 47 departed).

Because employee attrition represents a classic imbalanced classification problem (~16.1% positive rate), predicting the majority class trivially yields ~84% accuracy while failing to detect any at-risk employees. To solve this, all models incorporate **balanced class weighting** (`class_weight='balanced'`), prioritizing the capture of at-risk employees (Recall) while maintaining strong discriminative power (ROC-AUC).

### Genuine Evaluation Metrics Table

| Model Architecture | 5-Fold CV ROC-AUC | Test Accuracy | Test Precision | Test Recall (Sensitivity) | Test F1-Score | Test ROC-AUC |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Logistic Regression (Selected)** | **0.8282** | **75.51%** | **35.29%** | **63.83%** | **0.4545** | **0.8070** |
| **Random Forest Classifier** | 0.8071 | 82.99% | 47.17% | 53.19% | 0.5000 | 0.7891 |
| **Decision Tree Classifier** | 0.6742 | 76.19% | 33.80% | 51.06% | 0.4068 | 0.6469 |

### Why Logistic Regression was Selected as Champion
In predictive HR analytics, a **False Negative** (failing to recognize an at-risk employee who subsequently leaves) incurs a high replacement cost. Logistic Regression achieved the highest **Recall (63.83%)** and the highest **ROC-AUC (0.8070)**, detecting 30 out of 47 departing employees in the unseen test set, while maintaining smooth, calibrated probability outputs.

### Top Influential Predictive Features
1. `JobRole_Laboratory Technician`: Positive weight on flight risk.
2. `JobRole_Research Director`: Strong retention association.
3. `BusinessTravel_Non-Travel`: Decreases attrition likelihood.
4. `JobRole_Sales Representative`: High turnover risk.
5. `OverTime_No`: Significant protective retention factor.

## 9. Key Insights & Limitations

### Core Findings
- **Overtime Surge**: Employees working overtime depart at a rate of **30.5%**, compared to **10.4%** for non-overtime staff (~3x higher risk).
- **Role Disparities**: Sales Representatives experience the highest role turnover (**39.8%**), followed by Laboratory Technicians (**23.9%**) and Human Resources (**23.1%**).
- **Compensation Dynamics**: Departing personnel have a median monthly income of **$3,202** vs. **$5,204** for retained colleagues.
- **Tenure Risk Curve**: The highest attrition occurs within the first 12–24 months of joining an organization.

### Technical & Ethical Limitations
- **Synthetic Data**: As an IBM synthetic benchmark, the patterns may not generalize to specific specialized industry workforces.
- **Correlation ≠ Causation**: High overtime correlates with departure, but eliminating overtime will not automatically resolve underlying dissatisfaction or compensation issues.
- **Decision Support Only**: This tool is an assistive diagnostic, never an autonomous decision-maker.
